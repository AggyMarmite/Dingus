## What's new in Dingus 0.3.0

The first release under the new name (formerly Nimbus TV) – your settings move across automatically.

* **Pause & rewind live TV** – Space to pause, ← to go back, End to catch up. Buffered to disk, so memory use stays flat.
* **Reminders** – right-click a programme that hasn't started; a pop-up with a Watch button appears a minute before.
* **Find a programme** – search every channel's listings by title.
* **Channel editor** – rename, hide and reorder channels; kept across refreshes.
* **Picture & sound** – aspect ratio, crop black bars, audio sync, subtitle timing, night mode, preferred languages.
* **Parental PIN** – lock categories (adult ones automatically).
* **Plex** – sign in with a code, browse your server, pick up where you left off.
* **Backup & restore**, **sleep timer**, **themes & accent colours**, **start with Windows**, **keyboard shortcut list (?)**.
* **Installer and in-app updates** – future versions are offered inside Dingus.

**Install:** download `Dingus-Setup-0.3.0.exe` below and run it. No admin rights needed, and .NET is included.
